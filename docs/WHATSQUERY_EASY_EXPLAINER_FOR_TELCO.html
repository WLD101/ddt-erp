1. What is WhatsQuery?

WhatsQuery is a business communication and automation platform. It helps businesses manage customer calls, WhatsApp messages, leads, orders, appointments, and business records from one dashboard.

For a common business owner, WhatsQuery acts like a smart front desk. It can answer customers, collect details, record conversations, create follow-up tasks, and show all activity in a simple dashboard.

In simple words: a customer contacts a business, WhatsQuery understands the request, records the important information, and shows it to the business team.

Simple Product Flow

Customer calls or sends WhatsApp message
→
WhatsQuery AI receptionist responds
→
Lead, order, booking, or call log is saved
→
Business sees everything in dashboard

2. Why Telecom Operators Matter

WhatsQuery Voice needs reliable telecom connectivity for business phone calls. A telecom partner can provide SIP trunks, local phone numbers, call routing, and local network support.

WhatsQuery adds the business software layer on top of telecom connectivity. Together, the telco provides the reliable communication network, and WhatsQuery provides the customer-facing automation and business dashboard.

Telecom Partner Provides SIP trunk, local numbers, call routing, network reliability, CDR support, and hosting/data center options.
WhatsQuery Provides AI receptionist, customer records, lead capture, dashboard, reports, WhatsApp channel, and business workflows.

4. How a Phone Call Works

When a customer calls a business number, the telecom network sends the call to WhatsQuery through SIP routing. WhatsQuery identifies which business owns that number and starts the correct AI receptionist.

Phone Call Flow

Customer calls business number
→
Telco sends call through SIP trunk
→
WhatsQuery finds the correct business
AI receptionist answers
→
Important details are saved
→
Business team reviews dashboard

Example

A customer calls a restaurant and asks, "Can I book a table for 4 people tonight?"

WhatsQuery can collect the customer name, phone number, requested time, and party size. The system saves this as a table request for staff to confirm.

What Gets Saved?

  • Caller number
  • Call time and duration
  • Call summary
  • Lead, order, or booking request if applicable
  • Recording link if recording is enabled and allowed

5. How a WhatsApp Message Works

Many Pakistani businesses rely heavily on WhatsApp. WhatsQuery treats WhatsApp as a full customer service channel, not just a notification tool.

WhatsApp Flow

Customer sends WhatsApp message
→
WhatsQuery receives secure webhook
→
System identifies correct business
AI prepares reply using business data
→
Conversation is saved
→
Business sees customer chat

Important Safety Rule

WhatsQuery never uses one business's data to answer another business's customer. Every message is matched to the correct business account before any response is prepared.

Example

A customer messages a cafe: "Are you open today and can I order takeaway?"

WhatsQuery checks the cafe's business hours and order rules, replies with available information, and asks for customer details if the team needs to confirm the order.

6. How the AI Receptionist Knows What to Say

The AI receptionist does not guess business facts. Each business trains it using its own information.

Business ProfileName, industry, phone, website, branches, and basic details.
Opening HoursNormal timing, holiday timing, and after-hours behavior.
FAQsCommon questions and approved answers.
Services / MenuWhat the business offers, with notes and availability rules.
Booking RulesHow to capture appointment or table booking requests.
Handoff RulesWhen to ask staff or capture customer details.

AI Training Flow

Business adds profile, FAQs, services, rules
→
WhatsQuery builds safe AI instructions
→
AI answers customers within limits

What the AI Must Not Invent

  • Exact prices unless configured
  • Discounts unless configured
  • Confirmed availability unless backend/staff confirms
  • Medical, legal, or financial advice
  • Refund policy if not configured
  • Services or menu items not provided by the business

8. Data Separation and Privacy

WhatsQuery is designed for many businesses to use the same platform safely. Each business has its own account and its own data area.

Tenant Separation

Cafe Tenant
Cafe calls Cafe WhatsApp Cafe leads
Restaurant Tenant
Restaurant calls Restaurant WhatsApp Restaurant orders
Clinic Tenant
Clinic calls Clinic appointments Clinic callbacks

Plain-English Security Controls

  • Users must log in.
  • Users can only see their own business account.
  • Staff roles can be limited.
  • Admin-only areas are not visible to normal tenants.
  • Integration secrets are not shown back to users.
  • Backups and monitoring are planned for production safety.

10. What We Need from the Telecom Partner

To connect WhatsQuery Voice with telecom calling, we need SIP trunking and related technical information.

Requirement Why It Is Needed
SIP trunk To route customer phone calls into WhatsQuery.
Local numbers / DIDs To assign business phone numbers to customers.
Concurrent channels To decide how many calls can happen at the same time.
Static IP whitelisting To secure SIP traffic between telco and WhatsQuery.
Codec and RTP details To make audio work correctly.
CDR access To reconcile call duration, billing, and usage records.
SLA and support path To resolve production calling issues quickly.
Hosting/data center option To keep production telecom workloads inside Pakistan if required.
